# Humanize: One-Page Editing Checklist

Work top-down — structural edits first, line edits last. Full rationale in [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md).

## Pass 1 — Structure
- [ ] Delete the first paragraph; open with a concrete example, claim, or number
- [ ] Cut the conclusion, or replace with one forward-looking concrete fact
- [ ] Kill every sentence about the article itself ("In this section we will…", "Let's dive in")
- [ ] Collapse bullet-with-bold-header lists into prose where the content is reasoning
- [ ] De-parallel the headers; sentence-case them; delete header-restating warm-up lines
- [ ] Cut any "Challenges and Future Prospects" template section
- [ ] Let sections and paragraphs be unequal lengths

## Pass 2 — Claims and specificity (highest value)
- [ ] Every "experts say / studies show": name the source or delete the claim
- [ ] One number, name, date, or price per abstract claim
- [ ] One detail only you could know, per section (what broke, what it cost, what surprised you)
- [ ] Convert every pros-and-cons standoff into a verdict: who wins, under what conditions
- [ ] Where you don't know: say it isn't known — no plausible filler
- [ ] Name your examples ("Claude, Perplexity," not "AI tools")

## Pass 3 — Rhythm
- [ ] Three consecutive same-length sentences → cut one to ~6 words, stretch one past 30
- [ ] Add a one-sentence paragraph somewhere it earns it
- [ ] Break the tricolons: strongest item alone, or two items, or four
- [ ] One fragment for emphasis is fine; a run of dramatic fragments is a tell

## Pass 4 — Diction and constructions
- [ ] Sweep canon vocabulary: delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, seamless, robust, vibrant, leverage, foster, showcase, underscore, boasts, nestled, landscape/realm/journey (figurative), ever-evolving, game-changer
- [ ] Negative parallelism ("It's not just X, it's Y") → direct affirmative claim
- [ ] Amputate -ing tails ("…, highlighting the importance of…") → state the fact that shows it
- [ ] "serves as / stands as / boasts" → "is / has"
- [ ] One hedge max per claim; "It is important to note that" dies on sight
- [ ] Em-dash sweep: keep one that earns its interruption, convert the rest
- [ ] Strip mid-prose bold, emoji headers, Title Case Headings
- [ ] Normalize quotes/apostrophes; hunt hidden Unicode and stray markdown
- [ ] Delete chatbot residue ("I hope this helps!", "Certainly!")
- [ ] Un-cycle synonyms: repeat the clearest word

## Pass 5 — Voice
- [ ] State the take you actually hold
- [ ] Make uncertainty personal ("this bothers me and I can't explain why"), not institutional ("implications remain unclear")
- [ ] Admit one failure or mixed feeling
- [ ] Allow one tangent or parenthetical self-correction
- [ ] (Skip for reference/technical text — there, plain and neutral IS the human voice)

## Pass 6 — Read aloud
- [ ] Read it out loud; fix everything that doesn't sound like conversation
- [ ] Hopeless passage? Explain it to a friend, then write down what you said

## Final audit
- [ ] Ask: "What makes this still obviously AI-generated?" List residual tells; one more pass
- [ ] Cluster check: 3+ tell families remaining = not done (see GUIDE §7)
