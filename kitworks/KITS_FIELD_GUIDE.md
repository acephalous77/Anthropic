# KITWORKS field guide — 29 kits

Navigating the collection live. Every kit loads six section columns down one
707 clip column: **1 intro · 2 main · 3 lift · 4 break · 5 peak · 6 end** — so
scene 1–6 = intro/main/lift/break/peak/end, and the rides
`1-2-2-3 | 2-3-4-2 | 1-2-3-5-4-2-5-6` work on any of them.

Every kit has drums + bass + lead + counter + chords; a few add an arp.
`sw` = 8th-note shuffle on every part. Match a kit to the moment below, or
by key/tempo to chain two.

To add a kit: copy any `kits/kNN_*.py`, renumber, rewrite the notes; keep the
covenant (drums on pads 36–51, a hit on beat 1, every velocity deliberate,
parts written against each other). Then `python build.py && python tests.py`.
