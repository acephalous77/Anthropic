Ironveil  --  Am  126 bpm  feel:pushing   (lane: techno_steel)
======================================================================
TEMPO: the 707 has ONE tempo per project -- set it to 126 before playing (clips do NOT carry tempo).

TONES  (load one per track; TRK/CLP set to CLP lets each kit keep its
  own sound on a shared track -- map these to your factory/pack patches)
  drums    Kit: TR-909 (hard, dry)
  bass     Synth Bass: reese/detuned saw
  lead     Lead: hollow saw / acid
  counter  Synth: metallic blip
  chords   Synth: filtered stab

FIXED KNOBS  (same on every track; [SHIFT]+[KNOB ASSIGN] to bind)
  FILTER  -> Cutoff                       CC80 (or direct CC74)    start 90
  MOD     -> Coarse Tune                  CC81 (bind internally)   start 64
  FX      -> MFX depth / send (per part)  CC82                     start 40
  SOUND   -> Release                      CC83 (or direct CC72)    start 64
  (Coarse Tune has no direct CC -- bind MOD to the tone's Coarse Tune;
   the 4th knob's target is per-track, shown below.)

PER-TRACK INSERT MFX / SENDS / EQ / 4th-knob   (feel: pushing)
  drums
     MFX : Overdrive (Drive 24, Tone 66, Lvl 100) -- glue+edge
     send: delay   8   reverb  10     FX-knob -> MFX depth
     EQ  : HPF35; +2@70 +2.5@4k +1.5@11k (bright/punchy)
  bass
     MFX : Overdrive (Drive 40, Tone 55, Lvl 100) -- acid bite
     send: delay   0   reverb   4     FX-knob -> MFX depth (drive)
     EQ  : HPF28; +2@100 -2@350 roll>6k
  lead
     MFX : Stereo Delay (1/8dot, Fbk 30, HFdamp 7k, Lvl 40)
     send: delay  20   reverb  14     FX-knob -> Delay send
     EQ  : HPF120; +2.5@3k +1.5@10k (presence)
  counter
     MFX : Phaser (Rate 1/8, Depth 60, Reso 40) -- movement
     send: delay  16   reverb  12     FX-knob -> Delay send
     EQ  : HPF160; -1.5@3k +1@8k (yield to lead)
  chords
     MFX : JUNO Chorus (Dep 3, Rate .6Hz) + light OD (Drive 18)
     send: delay  10   reverb  22     FX-knob -> Reverb send
     EQ  : HPF180; -1.5@400 +2@10k air

MASTER BUS  ([SHIFT]+[MULTI] -> Comp / EQ / MFX tabs)
  Total Comp: 3.5:1, atk 12ms, rel 120ms/Auto, thr -13dB, +3.5 makeup (~3-4dB GR, punch)
  Total EQ:   low +1.5@60Hz . mid -1.5@400Hz . high +2@10kHz
  Total MFX: light Saturator/Overdrive (Drive 12-15) or Thru

MOTION  (arrangement filter/reverb arc on CC74 cutoff + CC91 reverb +
  CC11 expression: intro filtered, opens to peak, washes in the break,
  fades on the end). Live-record it with  usb_feed --fx --feel pushing,
  or ride the FILTER knob per column. Section cutoff/reverb targets:
    intro  cutoff x0.55  reverb x1.15  level 92
    main   cutoff x1.00  reverb x1.00  level 110
    lift   cutoff x1.15  reverb x1.10  level 120
    break  cutoff x0.45  reverb x1.60  level 84

    end    cutoff x0.80  reverb x1.35  level 100
