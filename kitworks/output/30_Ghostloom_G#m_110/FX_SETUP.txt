Ghostloom  --  G#m  110 bpm  feel:laidback   (lane: art_pop)
======================================================================
TEMPO: the 707 has ONE tempo per project -- set it to 110 before playing (clips do NOT carry tempo).

TONES  (load one per track; TRK/CLP set to CLP lets each kit keep its
  own sound on a shared track -- map these to your factory/pack patches)
  drums    Kit: hybrid (electronic+live)
  bass     Synth Bass: round moog
  lead     Synth: brooding saw
  counter  Voice: choir
  chords   Pad: wide analog

FIXED KNOBS  (same on every track; [SHIFT]+[KNOB ASSIGN] to bind)
  FILTER  -> Cutoff                       CC80 (or direct CC74)    start 90
  MOD     -> Coarse Tune                  CC81 (bind internally)   start 64
  FX      -> MFX depth / send (per part)  CC82                     start 40
  SOUND   -> Release                      CC83 (or direct CC72)    start 64
  (Coarse Tune has no direct CC -- bind MOD to the tone's Coarse Tune;
   the 4th knob's target is per-track, shown below.)

PER-TRACK INSERT MFX / SENDS / EQ / 4th-knob   (feel: laidback)
  drums
     MFX : Phonograph (Noise 22, Wow 14, warm) -- vinyl
     send: delay  18   reverb  30     FX-knob -> MFX depth
     EQ  : HPF30; +1.5@80 -1@3-4k roll>12k (round)
  bass
     MFX : Warm Saturator (Drive 24, Tone 48) -- warm
     send: delay   6   reverb  12     FX-knob -> MFX depth (drive)
     EQ  : HPF30; +2.5@80 -1.5@300 roll>5k
  lead
     MFX : Tape Echo (1/8, Fbk 42, Wow 20, Lvl 46) -- dubby
     send: delay  34   reverb  40     FX-knob -> Delay send
     EQ  : HPF110; +1.5@2k +1@8k -1@500
  counter
     MFX : JUNO Chorus + slap Stereo Delay (1/16, Fbk 12)
     send: delay  26   reverb  34     FX-knob -> Delay send
     EQ  : HPF150; -1@2.5k +1@9k air
  chords
     MFX : JUNO Chorus (Dep 4, Rate .4Hz) -- lush/wide
     send: delay  20   reverb  54     FX-knob -> Reverb send
     EQ  : HPF150; -1@350 +2.5@10k +1@220

MASTER BUS  ([SHIFT]+[MULTI] -> Comp / EQ / MFX tabs)
  Total Comp: 2.2:1, atk 28ms, rel 250ms, thr -16dB, +2 makeup (transparent, ~2dB GR)
  Total EQ:   low +2@80Hz . mid +1@250Hz . high +1@8kHz
  Total MFX: Warm Saturator (Drive 10) or Phonograph (subtle)

MOTION  (arrangement filter/reverb arc on CC74 cutoff + CC91 reverb +
  CC11 expression: intro filtered, opens to peak, washes in the break,
  fades on the end). Live-record it with  usb_feed --fx --feel laidback,
  or ride the FILTER knob per column. Section cutoff/reverb targets:
    intro  cutoff x0.55  reverb x1.15  level 92
    main   cutoff x1.00  reverb x1.00  level 110
    lift   cutoff x1.15  reverb x1.10  level 120
    break  cutoff x0.45  reverb x1.60  level 84
    peak   cutoff x1.30  reverb x1.20  level 126
    end    cutoff x0.80  reverb x1.35  level 100
