*2026-07-02 — covering the full endeavor: 11 packs, ~1,360 files, 20 modules*

## Audit findings (measured, not guessed)

### Musical — what the ear would notice

| # | Finding | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Album basses are homogeneous**: one builder gives every track the same root–fifth–root bar shape | only **4 distinct dominant bass-rhythm patterns across all 16 tracks** | high |

## The plan, in order

### Phase 1 — Make the album *sing* (musical, highest audible payoff)
1. **Per-track bass styles** (fixes M1): reuse stemlib's bass vocabulary (ostinato / ground / dub / pump / walking / acid) as a `bass=` field per track spec; grounds for the laments, dub for the shore, pump for the last dance.
2. **Wire swing** (M2): swung beat styles get `humanize.swing` in the album drum pipeline — one line, big feel difference at 72–96 BPM.
3. **Cadential endings** (M3): a `coda` section kind — final tonic chord in the pads, held low root in bass+drone ringing past the last bar, one last lead note on the tonic. The album's tracks should *land*, not evaporate.
4. **Fills & pickups** (M6): a real fill vocabulary (16th tom run, snare build, hat-choke stop) in the bar before each chorus; lead anacrusis (2–3 pickup notes) into choruses.
5. **Section dynamics** (M5): crescendo ramp across the last 2 bars of each verse; chorus enters ~15% hotter; bridge dips.

### Phase 2 — Motion the 707 can ride (M4)
6. **CC automation pass**: expression (CC11) swells on pads/drone per section arc; brightness (CC74) rising through bridges and final choruses; already supported by `write_track(cc_events=…)` — the plumbing exists, nothing uses it.

### Phase 3 — Consolidation (S1, S4)
7. **`songcraft.py`**: single home for `voice_into`, `lead_voicing`, `chord_of`, anchor/fold, the section-plan runner, and the spec builder. sophia.py and album.py become *data + character hooks* over one engine (byte-identity check on sophia vols 1–3 and the album before/after, same discipline as previous refactors).

### Phase 4 — Guardrails & findability (S2, S3)
8. **Promote validations into tests.py**: register clearance per stem type, sophia vocal-octave emptiness, album invariants (picardy G#, gap growth, 5/4 meter, anchors), stemlib determinism (double-run md5).
9. **Master catalog**: one `output/INDEX_ALL.csv` spanning every pack (pack, name, type, key, scale, bpm, meter, feel, length, seed, path), generated by make_everything so it can never omit a pack.

### Phase 5 — Library gaps (new material, after quality)
10. **Transitions pack**: fills, risers, turnarounds, stops — the connective tissue between clips that live sets need.
11. **Odd-meter slots** for stemlib (5/4, 7/8) feeding the Radiohead side.
12. **Album dub companion**: same 16 songs stripped to drums/bass/drone for singing over.

## Sequencing rationale
Phases 1–2 change what you *hear* this week and touch one file each. Phase 3 is
prerequisite hygiene before any further pack (a 4th helper copy is where bugs
breed — the vary_cell register-escape bug appeared twice for exactly this
reason). Phase 4 makes every past verification permanent. Phase 5 only after
the foundation stops moving.
